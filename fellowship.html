One crew trains the next.

Every Subject Medias crew has a screened lead, and every lead came up shadowing one. When you're ready, you lead, and you train whoever's next. That loop is what powers our coverage.

  • The games families book.

    Every creator we send to a game came up through the fellowship, so every crew knows the shot list, the brief and the rules before kickoff.

  • Your own creator brand.

    Your own page, QR codes for your events and sales of your work, then a team of your own. You climb as your work earns it.

  • The next class of creators.

    Leads train the shadows. The creators who come up through the fellowship are the ones who teach it next.

Screened before you're crewed.

Most of the athletes at our games are minors. These rules hold at every game, for every creator.

  • Three things on file.Your contract, a background check and minor-safety training. Each has to be less than a year old on the day of the game, or you can't be crewed.
  • 16 or 17? You shoot or shadow.You never lead or edit. You work beside a confirmed, screened adult, you're never alone with a minor, and you never sell to or message a family.
  • Under 18, a guardian signs on.A parent or guardian is on file with us, and your payouts go to their account.
  • The keep-out list comes first.Every crew brief lists the athletes you can't clip. Film the game as usual, but cut no clip that features them.
  • Consent before anything posts.Nothing with an athlete in it goes out without their consent.
