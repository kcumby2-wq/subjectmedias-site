In short

To confirm: every rule on this page. The rules below are a proposal: plain, fair to families, and easy for the team to apply.

If we don't cover your game, you get your money back. If you cancel early, you get your money back. If something's wrong with your photos or clips, we fix it or refund it. Email hello@subjectmedias.com from the address you paid with, and we'll reply within 24 hours.

Before the game

  • We can't staff your game: a full refund.
  • You cancel 7 or more days before: a full refund.
  • You cancel 2 to 6 days before: a credit for the full amount toward another game in the next 12 months, or a 50% refund.
  • You cancel less than 2 days before: no refund, because a creator has kept that day for you. If it's an emergency, write to us.

On game day

  • The game is postponed (weather, the league, the venue): we move your booking to the new date at no charge. If we can't make the new date, a full refund.
  • The event is cancelled outright: a full refund, or a credit if you'd rather.
  • Our creator doesn't make it: a full refund.
  • The venue or league won't allow filming: a full refund if we didn't know about the rule; a credit if it was a rule we were told about in time and still couldn't work around.
  • Your athlete doesn't play (an injury, a benching): a credit for another game.

After delivery

  • Something's wrong with the files (missing, broken, the wrong athlete): tell us within 7 days and we'll fix it; if we can't, a refund for what's wrong.
  • Late delivery: To confirm: a credit or a partial refund when delivery runs well past 48 hours, and how much
  • Once photos and clips are delivered and right, the package is done and isn't refundable.

Clips from event pages

  • Wrong athlete, or a file that won't play or download: we fix it or refund that clip.
  • The family keeps the athlete out after you bought: we close the download and refund that clip.
  • Otherwise clips are final once you've downloaded them.

Player transcripts

A full refund until the review of the film starts. After that, it isn't refundable, because the work is done for you.

The creator fellowship

Plans are month to month. Cancel any time: you keep your seat to the end of the month you've paid for, and the next month isn't charged. We don't refund part of a month.

How refunds work

Email hello@subjectmedias.com from the address you paid with. Refunds go back to the card you paid with, through Stripe; your bank usually shows them within 5 to 10 business days. Credits are good for 12 months.
