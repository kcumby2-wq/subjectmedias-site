Who we are

Subject Medias films and photographs youth and high school sports and delivers the footage to athletes' families, teams and events. We run subjectmedias.com. In this policy, "we" means Subject Medias, To confirm: the business's legal name (an LLC, or Kyron Cumby as a sole proprietor) and a mailing address.

This policy says what we collect, why, who helps us handle it, how long we keep it and what you can ask us to do. Questions go to hello@subjectmedias.com.

What we collect, and why

When you book coverage

The package and sport; the event's name, date, city and state; the team and jersey number of the athlete you're booking for; how many athletes; your name, email and phone; and anything you write in the notes. We never ask for an athlete's name: on our side an athlete is a team and a jersey number. We use these details to plan the game, match a creator to it and reach you about it. We email you one note: that we got your request or, if you went on to pay, thanks for booking.

When you pay

Payments run through Stripe. We never see or store your card number. Stripe tells us your name, email and phone (if you give it), what you bought and what you paid, and your answers to the checkout's questions. Answers about the athlete themselves, like a name or an age, stay with Stripe and don't come to us.

After you buy a package

Our details page asks where and when the game is: the same event details as the booking form, a phone number if you want us to text, and notes. We use them the same way.

Event clips pages

At events we cover, families decide whether and how an athlete's footage may be used: kept out, for their family only, sold to people with the event's access code, or also shown publicly, like on our store and social pages. For that we collect the athlete's team and jersey number, the choice, the athlete's age band (12 or younger, 13 to 15, 16 or 17, or 18 or older), who's answering (a parent or legal guardian, or the athlete as an adult) and the email of the person answering, so we can confirm the choice and send the family's link. When you buy clips, we collect your email and the order to deliver your downloads.

Footage

We film and photograph games, so our footage shows athletes, many of them minors, as well as coaches, officials and fans. We show or sell an athlete's footage only as their family chose. Athletes 12 or younger are for their family only: never sold to others or shown publicly. A keep-out takes effect right away, from anyone. To confirm: that, without a family's choice to go public, any footage of minors we post publicly is a wide shot that doesn't single anyone out

Jersey numbers read by AI

To suggest which athletes are in a clip, we send still frames from it to Anthropic's AI service, which reads the jersey numbers. A person on our team checks every match before a clip is shown to a family or sold. Counsel: confirm that Anthropic's commercial terms (no training on our data, its retention) are described accurately here

Fellowship applications

Your name, email, phone, Instagram handle, city and state, age group, the seat and plan you're interested in, links to your work, your gear and what you tell us. Applicants who are 16 or 17 apply with a parent or guardian, so we also collect that person's name, email and phone. We use all of it to review your application and to reach you about it.

Visits to our pages

We count how many times a day our pages are opened and how often people open the booking form, tap a Buy button or see our 10% offer. We keep these as totals for each day and nothing else: no cookie, no record of who you are, your address or your device. If your browser sends Global Privacy Control, we don't count your visit at all.

Like every website, the companies that host ours see your device's IP address when your browser connects, to deliver the page and to stop abuse. We use that address to limit how often a form can be sent from one place, without storing it.

Email

When you write to us, or we write to you, those messages stay in our email account.

Cookies and your browser

subjectmedias.com doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser keeps a few small notes on your own device that never come to us: that you've seen our 10% offer card, so it doesn't show again; that you claimed the offer, for that browser tab; and that you already sent the details for a game.

Our pages load their fonts from Google Fonts, so Google sees your IP address when your browser fetches them. To confirm: serving the fonts from our own site instead, so no visit reaches Google

Who we share it with

Companies that run parts of our service for us, and only to run them:

  • Stripe takes payments.
  • Vercel hosts subjectmedias.com.
  • Render runs our booking and clips app.
  • Supabase holds our database and our footage files.
  • Google runs our email account and serves our fonts.
  • Anthropic reads jersey numbers in still frames, as described above.
  • To confirm: an email sending service, like Postmark or Amazon SES, if we move our emails to one

The creator who covers your game gets what they need to do it: the event's details and the team and jersey number. To confirm: whether creators also get the booker's phone or email to coordinate on game day

We sell clips of an athlete only when their family chose to allow it, and never for athletes 12 or younger. We don't sell your personal information otherwise, and we don't share it for targeted advertising. Counsel: whether selling one family's athlete's clips to others with the event's access code counts as a sale or sharing of that athlete's personal information under state privacy laws, and what notice or opt-in it needs

We may also share information when the law requires it, to protect someone's safety, or with a buyer if the business is sold, who would have to keep this policy's promises.

Children and teens

Booking, buying and choosing how an athlete's footage is used are for adults: a parent or legal guardian for an athlete under 18, or an athlete who is 18 or older. We don't knowingly collect personal information online directly from children under 13. If you think a child under 13 has given us their information, email hello@subjectmedias.com and we'll delete it.

Fellowship applicants are 16 or older. An applicant who is 16 or 17 applies with a parent or guardian, whose name and email are on the application.

Counsel: review the children's sections against COPPA and the state laws on minors' data, given that families give us information about athletes under 13 and we film them

How long we keep it

To confirm: how long each kind of record is kept. Proposed:

  • Bookings and payments: 7 years, for tax and accounting records.
  • The notes and details of a booking, beyond what those records need: 2 years after the game.
  • Footage: 2 years after the event, unless the family asks us to delete it sooner, or chose to keep it public.
  • Families' choices about footage: as long as we keep that footage, plus 3 years, so we can show what was agreed.
  • Clip orders: 7 years, like other payments. Download links stay in the order email; each download is signed for an hour.
  • Fellowship applications: 1 year after we decide, or for as long as you're in the fellowship.
  • Daily visit counts: as long as they're useful. They hold no personal information.

Your choices

  • See, fix or delete your information. Email hello@subjectmedias.com from the address you used with us. We'll answer within 45 days, and we may ask a question or two to make sure it's you. Records the law makes us keep, like payments, we keep for as long as it says.
  • Change how an athlete's footage is used. Use the link in your family email, or email us. A keep-out takes effect right away and ends sales of that athlete's clips.
  • Emails from us. We email you about your booking, order or application. We don't send marketing emails.

Depending on where you live, state law may give you more rights, like knowing what we have or appealing our answer. Email us, and we won't treat you differently for asking. Counsel: which state laws reach a business our size, and whether they need more here

Keeping it safe

Our database and footage files are private to our app's servers. The people on our team sign in to reach them and see only what their work needs. Payments stay with Stripe. Download links for clips are signed and work for an hour at a time. No system is perfect: if you see something wrong, email hello@subjectmedias.com.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the new one here with its date. If a change matters, we'll also tell the people we have an email for before it takes effect.
